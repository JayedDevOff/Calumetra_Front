Paycheck Calculator

Our free Paycheck Calculator helps you estimate your take-home pay after taxes and deductions. Enter your gross salary, choose your pay frequency, select your currency, and adjust your tax rates to calculate your estimated net pay.

How to Calculate Take-Home Pay

Take-home pay is calculated by subtracting taxes and other deductions from your gross pay. Enter your gross income and applicable deductions above to get an estimate of the amount you may receive after deductions.

What Is Gross Pay?

Gross pay is the total amount you earn before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, and other deductions are taken out.

What Is Net Pay?

Net pay is the amount remaining after taxes and other deductions. It is commonly known as take-home pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a paycheck calculator?

A paycheck calculator estimates your take-home pay by subtracting taxes and other deductions from your gross income.

What is the difference between gross pay and net pay?

Gross pay is your total earnings before deductions, while net pay is the amount you receive after taxes and other deductions.

Can I calculate weekly and monthly pay?

Yes. You can calculate weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, monthly, or annual pay.

Does this calculator support multiple currencies?

Yes. You can select from multiple international currencies including USD, BDT, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD, JPY, AED, SAR, and more.
