Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

i

How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. To calculate a percentage, divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100.

For example, if you have 50 out of 200: (50 ÷ 200) × 100 = 25%.
