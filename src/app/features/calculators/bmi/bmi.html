01

What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index, commonly called BMI, is a calculation based on a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening measure to classify weight status in adults.

BMI does not directly measure body fat and should not be considered a diagnosis. Factors such as muscle mass, age, body composition, and overall health can affect how BMI should be interpreted.

02

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

If you use feet, inches, or pounds, this calculator converts the measurements internally before calculating BMI.

Should You Gain or Lose Weight?

If your BMI is below 18.5, the calculator identifies your weight as below the general adult BMI reference range. The estimated difference shows how much weight would theoretically bring you closer to the lower end of that range.

If your BMI is between 18.5 and 24.9, your weight is within the general adult BMI reference range and the calculator recommends maintaining your current weight.

If your BMI is 25 or higher, the calculator estimates the difference between your current weight and the upper end of the general healthy BMI range.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about BMI calculations and weight guidance.

What is a healthy BMI for adults?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly used as the general healthy BMI reference range for adults.

Does BMI tell me exactly how much body fat I have?

No. BMI is a screening measure based on height and weight. It does not directly measure body fat or body composition.

What if my BMI is below 18.5?

A BMI below 18.5 falls below the general adult BMI reference range. The calculator provides an estimated weight difference, but individual nutritional and health needs can vary.

What if my BMI is above 25?

A BMI of 25 or higher falls above the general adult BMI reference range. The calculator provides an estimated difference from the upper end of the range, but it should not be treated as a medical weight-loss prescription.

Can I use pounds and feet?

Yes. Select ft & in for height and lb for weight. The calculator converts these measurements internally before calculating BMI.
