WHAT IS COMPOUND INTEREST?

Compound interest is interest earned on both your original investment and the interest that has already accumulated. Over longer periods, this can significantly increase the growth of an investment.

WHY REGULAR CONTRIBUTIONS MATTER

Adding money regularly allows each contribution to potentially earn interest over time. Increasing the contribution amount or investment period can have a substantial effect on the final balance.
