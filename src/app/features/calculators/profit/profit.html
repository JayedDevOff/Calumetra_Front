FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about profit margins and business profitability.

What is a profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of revenue that remains as profit after deducting costs and expenses.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin measures profit as a percentage of selling price, while markup measures profit as a percentage of cost.

What is gross profit?

Gross profit is revenue minus the direct cost of goods or services sold. It does not include operating expenses.

What is net profit?

Net profit is the amount remaining after deducting both direct costs and operating expenses from revenue.
