HOW TO CALCULATE INCOME TAX

UNDERSTAND THE BASIC TAX CALCULATION PROCESS.

01

TAXABLE INCOME

Taxable income is generally calculated by reducing gross income by deductions allowed under the applicable country's tax rules.

TAXABLE INCOME = GROSS INCOME − ELIGIBLE DEDUCTIONS
02

INCOME TAX

Progressive tax systems divide taxable income into brackets. Each portion of income is taxed at the rate applicable to that bracket.

TAX = SUM OF TAX ACROSS APPLICABLE BRACKETS
03

NET INCOME

Take-home income is calculated after applicable income tax, social contributions, regional taxes, deductions and eligible credits.

NET = GROSS − TAX − CONTRIBUTIONS + CREDITS
