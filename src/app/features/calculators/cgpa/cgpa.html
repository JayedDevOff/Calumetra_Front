How to Calculate CGPA

CGPA is calculated by dividing total quality points by total completed credits. Quality points are calculated by multiplying each course's credit by its corresponding grade point.

CGPA Formula CGPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credits

How to Calculate GPA

GPA is calculated for a semester or group of courses by dividing the total quality points by the total credits attempted during that period.

GPA Formula GPA = Semester Quality Points ÷ Semester Credits

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about GPA and CGPA calculation.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents the credit-weighted average of grade points earned across multiple semesters or academic periods.

What is GPA?

GPA is the Grade Point Average for a particular semester or group of courses.

How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is calculated by dividing total quality points by total credits. Quality points are obtained by multiplying course credits by grade points.

Can I add multiple semesters?

Yes. Add semesters dynamically and enter the courses completed in each semester. The calculator automatically combines all semester results into your overall CGPA.

Can I add courses one by one?

Yes. Each semester starts with one course and you can add as many additional subjects as required.

Is CGPA a simple average of semester GPAs?

No. A correct cumulative CGPA should normally be credit-weighted. Semester quality points and credits are combined to calculate the overall result.

Can I use a 5.00 or 10.00 grading scale?

Yes. The calculator supports 4.00, 5.00 and 10.00 grading scales.

Are GPA conversions officially accurate?

Not necessarily. GPA and CGPA conversion policies vary by university, country and credential evaluation authority. Conversion results from this calculator are proportional estimates.
