🔒 Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

At Calumetra, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how cookies and similar technologies may be used, and how we protect information when you use our website and online calculators.

↻ Last updated: September 5, 2026
01

About Calumetra

Calumetra is an online calculator platform that provides free tools for everyday calculations, including age, BMI, percentage, finance and other useful calculations.

You can use most Calumetra calculators without creating an account or providing personal information.

02

Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the information that is reasonably necessary to operate, maintain and improve Calumetra.

Information You Enter Into Calculators

Calculator inputs such as dates, numbers, height, weight, income, loan amounts or other calculation values may be entered by you.

Unless specifically stated otherwise on a particular calculator, these values are used to perform the requested calculation and are not intended to identify you personally.

Information You Provide Directly

If you contact us through email, a contact form or another communication method, we may receive information such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit Calumetra, certain technical and usage information may automatically be collected by our website or third-party services. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Referring website or page
  • General website usage information
03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating Calumetra's calculator tools
  • Improving website functionality and user experience
  • Understanding how visitors use our website
  • Monitoring website performance and security
  • Responding to questions and support requests
  • Detecting and preventing abuse or fraudulent activity
  • Maintaining and improving our services
  • Supporting website advertising and analytics where applicable
04

Calculator Data and User Inputs

Calumetra is designed primarily for calculations performed directly in your browser. Calculator results are generated based on the information you enter.

We do not intentionally require personal information simply to use our standard calculators.

Where calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser, the information entered may remain on your device and be used only to generate the requested result.

Some features or future services may operate differently. Where a feature requires information to be collected or processed, appropriate notice will be provided.

05

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Calumetra may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage, support security and performance, and provide advertising services where applicable.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Remembering website preferences
  • Website analytics
  • Security and performance
  • Measuring website usage
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements

Advertising Cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google may use advertising cookies to help serve relevant advertisements and measure advertising performance. The use of advertising cookies may depend on applicable consent requirements and the advertising settings available for the user.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

06

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors interact with Calumetra. Analytics information helps us identify technical issues, improve our pages and understand which calculators are most useful.

Analytics providers may collect information such as device information, browser information, pages visited, approximate location and general usage information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Analytics information is generally used in an aggregated or statistical manner to help us improve the website.

Advertising

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, measure or improve advertisements.

Where required by applicable law, advertising and cookie technologies may be used only after obtaining the appropriate user consent.
